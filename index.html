FAQ

Is ClipKaro free?

Yes. It runs entirely in your browser, so there are no server costs.

Which languages work?

Language is detected automatically: Hindi, English and 90+ others. Hinglish is transcribed on a best-effort basis, and you can edit any caption before exporting.

Is my video uploaded?

No. Transcription and video rendering happen on your device.

Why does exporting take as long as the video?

Clips are rendered in real time in your browser for best compatibility. A 10-minute video takes about 10 minutes to export. Keep the tab open and visible.

What format are the clips?

MP4 in recent Chrome and Edge, otherwise WebM. Both upload fine to Instagram, YouTube Shorts and Facebook.
